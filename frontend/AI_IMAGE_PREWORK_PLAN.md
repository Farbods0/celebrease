# 🎨 CeleBrease Multi-Angle AI Image Generation & Execution Plan

## 📋 Overview
This plan establishes the automated generation of **4 unique AI photography angles** for every tier across all 11 newly integrated holidays (44 total unique image assets), curated and refined by Master Designer.

---

## 📸 Camera Angle Specifications per Kit Tier
For each holiday, 4 distinct camera angles are generated:
1. **Angle 1 (`angle1`) - The Hero Room / Fireplace Mantel View**: Architectural/interior digest shot capturing full ambient luxury setting and 2700K lighting.
2. **Angle 2 (`angle2`) - The Tabletop & Centerpiece Detail**: Medium close-up focusing on table runner, place settings, candles, and centerpiece composition with f/2.8 bokeh.
3. **Angle 3 (`angle3`) - Accent & Texture Close-up**: Macro/close-up highlighting premium materials (velvet pumpkins, brass Kiddush cups, embroidered silk, crystal glassware).
4. **Angle 4 (`angle4`) - Overhead / Flat-lay Product Composition**: Top-down studio flat-lay showing all included items laid out cohesively on marble or slate.

## 🛠️ Automated Execution Script Plan
A Node.js script (`execute_all_tier_images.js`) will:
1. Iterate over all 11 holidays and 4 angles (44 image prompts).
2. Call `generate_image` for each prompt.
3. Save output image files to `frontend/public/uploads/holidays/[slug]-[tier]-angle[1-4].jpg` and `backend/uploads/holidays/[slug]-[tier]-angle[1-4].jpg`.
4. Update the `images` array for each kit tier in PostgreSQL (`neondb`).
5. Run a full Playwright verification pass to confirm gallery rendering across all tiers.
